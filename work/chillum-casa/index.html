Chillum Casa

A marketplace for independent chillum makers and collectors—combining a considered editorial identity with accounts, catalogue workflows, moderation and commerce infrastructure.

01 / The brief

A home for
the craft.

The challenge was not simply to put products online. It was to create a credible digital home where singular handmade pieces, their makers and the mechanics of a marketplace could coexist.

Make the marketplace feel like a collection—not a catalogue template.

Chillum Casa connects independent artisans with collectors while preserving the identity and provenance of each piece.

OMLAYER shaped the product from brand-facing experience through to authentication, maker workflows, catalogue management, image infrastructure and founder moderation.

02 / Product scope

Two sides.
One Casa.

Public discovery and private maker operations run on the same underlying product model.

01

Artisan profiles

02

03

03 / System design

Editorial outside.
Systems inside.

The visible experience stays restrained while the operational layers handle identity, content, media and marketplace state.

Craft in front.
Infrastructure behind.

A Cloudflare-native architecture keeps the product compact: the application and Worker layer orchestrate workflows, D1 holds structured marketplace data, and R2 stores the original and optimized imagery that gives every piece its presence.

04 / What mattered

Built to
grow carefully.

The platform is approaching launch. The architecture is intentionally additive so real makers and real inventory can shape what comes next.

A small marketplace still deserves real product architecture.

Existing listings remain compatible as the schema evolves. Image associations, moderation history and product metadata were introduced without destructive rewrites.

The result is a foundation that can move from a curated first collection toward a broader maker marketplace without replacing the core system.
