Make a postcard from somewhere real or somewhere imagined. Print it, write on it, send it. No account needed.

Artwork is procedural, not AI-generated. Prompts steer colors, scenery and composition; they do not generate literal scenes. Photos and messages stay on this device unless you export or share them.

02 / Make it physical

Ask for one proof first. Print actual size (100%), no fit-to-page; double-sided alignment and trimming depend on the printer. Confirm local postal requirements before mailing.

03 / Find a printer

Opens Google Maps with your chosen area or coordinates; Maps supplies results, distances and opening hours. Call ahead to confirm cardstock and duplex printing.
