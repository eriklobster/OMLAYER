## Executive conclusion

There is **no automatic Thai visa/work-permit exemption** arising from:
- Creating a foreign LLC/Ltd, hosting the agency in the cloud, using AI agents, or receiving USD/EUR/USDT offshore.
- Delegating technical production to another country **while still actively managing, selling, or supervising from Thailand**.
- Rebranding a cannabis dispensary ad as a *physical-shop* or *lifestyle* ad.
- Using a person who does not genuinely work or a nominee/false owner. Never do this.

**Potentially lawful model requiring legal review:** Real overseas entity with truthful beneficial ownership, an actual overseas authorized operator who independently contracts, communicates, controls delivery, and supervises permitted work. A person visiting Thailand limits activities to tourism, passive ownership, and specifically permitted incidental activities. Legal advice must test repeated introductions, negotiations, client support, campaign approval and company-direction facts; an occasional meeting exemption is not an ongoing work permit.

## Work authorization

- The Thai SME office explains that visitor/tourist visa holders are not authorized to work.
- The Thai government lists narrow non-work activities, including attending a business meeting, visiting exhibitions, and visiting one's own company's board meeting. These exemptions must not be stretched to recurring client-facing work, delivery, managing AI agents, or paid local agency activity.
- Guidance on short urgent tasks is a *separate notification regime*, **not** an informal permission to perform an ongoing services contract.
- The test involves actions actually performed **while physically in Thailand**; foreign billing does not change the location of performance.

## Tax and cross-border business exposure

1. Thai Revenue Code **Section 76 Bis** can deem a foreign company to be carrying on business in Thailand when it has an employee, agent, or go-between conducting business there and thereby receives Thai income; possible local tax filing/liability. Confirm treaty and actual fact pattern with a Thai accountant.
2. Thai personal income guidance treats **work/business performed in Thailand** as Thai-source even if paid offshore.
3. For foreign-source income, the Thai Revenue Department's published guidance uses a **180-day presence** threshold in the relevant tax year and Thai remittance conditions. Check actual days and any changes/treaties with a tax advisor.
4. Cross-border service invoices to a Thai payer may create withholding, VAT, and documentation issues depending on actual transaction, treaty and presence; do not guess a universal rate.

Official sources:
- Thai Revenue Code Section 76 Bis: https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html

## Cannabis, Instagram and paid advertising

- Thailand restricted cannabis flower to licensed medical channels in 2025; the Thai government says online sales and all forms of cannabis advertising are prohibited. A fresh **2026-11-09** government update describes active enforcement against commercial promotions and online sales.
- Instagram has historically allowed some lawful cannabis-business **non-sale** informational pages while prohibiting sale inducement; this is **not Thai-law clearance**, and account removal is a real operational risk.
- Meta's paid advertising standards restrict THC/cannabis product promotion, including indirect promotions. An ad encouraging visits to a dispensary is not automatically compliant simply because it omits photos of flower, strains, prices or order links.
- **No paid dispensary campaigns, cannabis-themed influencer promotions, product pricing/posts, freebies-for-reviews or disguised funnels** absent written Thai legal and platform clearance.
- Separate cafés/hospitality businesses may have genuine independently marketable products, but only where real and not a proxy to drive cannabis sales.
- Work the overseas contractor can potentially undertake **after review**: factual nonpromotional business-profile administration, staff schedules, compliance record systems, independently permitted café photography/food information. An Instagram page for a dispensary needs content-by-content advice.

## Wise Business: major practical blocker

The **worldwide** Wise Acceptable Use Policy last updated **2026-03-25** lists cannabis and **other products or services related to legal marijuana trade** under restricted businesses/transactions. Marketing, branding, web support and administrative services for a cannabis dispensary may fall within this phrase even when invoice says "digital services". Transparent disclosure and **written provider confirmation** are necessary; do not mislabel the customer/service to pass checks. Other banking rails require independent underwriting; do not assume stablecoin payments are legal/accepted.

Wise Business *Thailand* onboarding (as of verification) supports freelancers and sole proprietors but not Thai limited companies; foreign residents need legitimate right-to-live documents, and supported business features differ by jurisdiction.

## Suggested decision sequence (zero unnecessary registrations)

1. **Classify product**: Is this merely factual profile administration, or is it a paid ad / sales funnel to a dispensary? For dispensary-directed ads assume not permitted.
2. **Check who really does what**: The overseas person has real authority, runs tools/AI agents, owns deliverables and legally contracts; the visiting shareholder does not manage work from Thailand. Document responsibilities honestly, not as a cover story.
3. **Ask a Thai lawyer** for a written opinion on (a) visitor's proposed activities, (b) promotional vs factual page content, (c) an offshore agency serving Thai dispensaries.
4. **Ask payment provider in writing** about a foreign digital agency providing admin/social-profile services to a licensed Thai cannabis store. Do not accept payment through Wise until approved.
5. **Select incorporation jurisdiction only if necessary** after Thai legal, provider, overseas manager and cross-border tax feasibility are understood.
6. **Only then** prepare lawful nonpromotional scope, agreement, invoice and client-controlled Instagram permissions. No false staff, fabricated businesses, or account credential sharing.
7. Keep confidential client communications and strategy in a private workspace, not this public repository.

**Open questions:** whether any overseas entity already exists; the overseas contractor's country and authority; actual client licences and scope; current payment-provider written eligibility; Thai lawyer view on client introductions and account setup. These are diligence items, not assumptions.

**Precisely scoped Thai government guidance:**
- The Thai government explicitly lists attending meetings or seminars, **business visits or negotiations**, and attending one's own company board meetings among activities that do not require a work permit on tourist status. [Thai government explanation, updated 2025-05-28](https://www.thailand.go.th/issue-focus-detail/7--work-permit--tourist-visa-?hl=en).
- The [BOI work-permit guidance](https://www.boi.go.th/index.php?language=en&page=legal_issues_for_investors_02_work_permits) distinguishes **visiting** a business site or **participating** in a meeting from organizing the visit or meeting; the government list should not be interpreted as a general permission to carry on continuing trade or employment in Thailand.
- Incidental social exchange of business cards is **not expressly settled by these sources**, and whether repeatedly approaching local businesses amounts to work depends on facts, purpose and pattern. Do not claim that a tourist can freely engage in recurring client acquisition.
- Genuine periodic **board/shareholder-level decisions** are different from providing day-to-day executive management. CEO title alone does not exempt ongoing CEO duties. Remote approval of deliverables, setting campaigns, supervising developers/AI agents, or handling routine Thai-customer support from Thailand creates work-authorization risk.
- A Thai lawyer should evaluate this exact activity matrix and visa stamp rather than treating “on holiday” as a legal exemption.
