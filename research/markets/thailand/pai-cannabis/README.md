## Research questions
- What are the current Google Maps rating, review count, newest-review date and common positive/negative themes for each shop and other Pai competitors?

- Which flower/menu prices, bundles, pre-roll/joint prices or promotions are publicly evidenced? Record **THB, date, source and confidence**, not guessed prices.
- Do reviews *report* a joint/discount in exchange for a review? If so, when and by whom, and is the claim independently corroborated? Never infer that an offer is still available. Do not propose review incentives.
- Are there verifiable links between differently named shops through **public legal entities or official business disclosures**, without inferring private persons or ownership from speculation?
- What legal non-cannabis-promotional services (e.g., permitted café/food branding if genuinely separate, internal admin/compliance systems) might OMLAYER offer, subject to professional review?
