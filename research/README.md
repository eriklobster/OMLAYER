# OMLAYER — Market Research Library

**Status:** working research library; **not** public-facing marketing copy or confirmed client claims.
**Owner approval:** required before any website publication, outreach, campaigns, or production changes.
**Repository visibility:** this repository is **public**. Store only public-source links and neutral, attributed research summaries. Do not commit private proposals, customer lists, full scraped reviews, unverified personal ownership allegations, contracts, credentials, screenshots of private accounts, or nonpublic client intelligence.

## Markets
- [Pai, Thailand — cannabis retail and adjacent hospitality](./markets/thailand/pai-cannabis/README.md) — discovery ongoing as of 2026-11-21.

## Evidence standard
- Keep original URL, retrieval date (Asia/Bangkok), publication/review date (if available), source type, and whether a claim was independently verified.
- Distinguish `VERIFIED`, `REPORTED`, `HISTORICAL`, `UNVERIFIED`, `DISPUTED`, and `NEEDS_RECHECK`.
- Store paraphrased review themes and aggregated counts **only with direct source links**. Do not systematically reproduce reviewers' text, personal identities, profiles or location history.
- Prices and promotions are time-dependent. Record THB and observation date. Never describe a historic deal as currently available without fresh verification.
- Shared ownership claims need public registration or an explicit official business statement; similar naming, staff, photos and customer rumors are insufficient.
- Keep legal/platform rules separated from observed competitor behavior. Do not assume an online cannabis promotion is lawful because it is visible.

## Workflow
1. Collect links and public evidence in a market folder.
2. Compare Google Maps with shop-controlled sites, Instagram/Facebook, independent reviews, and official registers as available.
3. Label all unknowns honestly, including access limitations.
4. Validate any client-facing proposal for Thai work authorization, cannabis marketing rules, Meta policy and approved payment channels.
5. Route confidential material to an approved **private** workspace. No automatic sync from ChatGPT Deep Research to GitHub is configured.

*Research content is for analysis, not approval to advertise or sell cannabis.*
